Keycraft · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Applies to the Keycraft keyboard app for Android

The short version

  • Keycraft does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account.
  • Your settings, clipboard history and background photos are kept only on your device, and you can delete them at any time.

01Who we are

Keycraft (“the app”) is a keyboard for Android developed by Bee MACH (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and what control you have over it. If you have any question, write to beemach.dev@gmail.com.

02What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers for the app, and the app has no permission to access the internet. We never receive your keystrokes, messages, clipboard, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage statistics.

03What the keyboard handles on your device

To work as a keyboard, Keycraft processes some information locally on your phone. None of it leaves the device.

Text you type

Keystrokes are processed in memory, as you type, to insert characters, build Vietnamese letters and tone marks (Telex and VNI), apply Unicode text styles and show word suggestions. Suggestions come from a word list built into the app. Keycraft does not save what you type, does not learn from it and does not send it anywhere.

When you enable any third-party keyboard, Android shows a standard warning that the keyboard “may be able to collect all the text you type, including personal data like passwords and credit card numbers”. Android shows this for every keyboard. Keycraft only uses your keystrokes as described above and has no network access to send them through.

Clipboard history

When the keyboard opens, Keycraft reads the text currently on your clipboard and adds it to a clipboard history so you can paste it again later. The history keeps up to 50 recent items plus any items you pin, and is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. You can delete single items or clear the history from the clipboard panel. If you copy sensitive text such as a password, it may appear in this history; delete it from the panel when you no longer need it.

Background photos

If you choose a photo as your keyboard background, Android’s photo picker gives Keycraft access to that one photo only. The app saves a resized copy in its private storage to draw the keyboard. Keycraft does not request access to your photo library or files.

Font Art images

When you create a Font Art image, it is generated on your device and stored temporarily in the app’s cache. It is shared only with the app you are typing in, at your request, through Android’s standard sharing mechanism.

Settings

Your chosen theme, keyboard design, languages, favorite themes, “My symbols” list and preferences (sound, vibration, key popup, suggestions) are stored in the app’s private storage.

04Permissions

PermissionWhy
VIBRATEHaptic feedback when you press a key. You can turn it off in the keyboard settings.
BIND_INPUT_METHODA system permission that lets Android run Keycraft as a keyboard. It is not something the app requests from you.

Keycraft does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or storage permissions.

05Third-party services

Keycraft contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Emoji and fonts are bundled inside the app and do not download anything.

06Android backups

If you have turned on Android’s backup to your Google Account, Android may include Keycraft’s app data (such as settings, clipboard history and background photos) in that backup and restore it to a new device. This backup is performed by Android and Google, not by us, and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

07Your choices

  • Delete clipboard items or clear the clipboard history from the clipboard panel.
  • Remove all app data in Android Settings → Apps → Keycraft → Storage → Clear data.
  • Switch to another keyboard at any time, or uninstall Keycraft to remove all of its data from your device.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You are always welcome to contact us with questions.

08Security

Everything the app keeps is stored in its private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Since the app has no network access, nothing is transmitted.

09Children

Keycraft is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of information changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs internet access, we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
